01Welcome

Welcome to the IT & Cyber Security Ecosystem.

This initiative is designed to bring together people who are interested in technology, cybersecurity, AI, data, automation and technology entrepreneurship — and help them grow through learning, practical projects, teamwork, service and leadership.

We are not building a traditional IT department where people simply receive tasks. We are building an ecosystem where:

People learn → People build → People serve → People lead → Projects grow → Products emerge → Ventures scale.

Whether you are an experienced IT professional or someone who is just beginning your journey, there can be a place for you — provided that you are willing to learn, contribute and take responsibility.

02What Are We Building?

Our long-term vision is to develop an IT & Cyber Security ecosystem that can:

  • Develop technical talent
  • Create practical learning opportunities
  • Build real technology projects
  • Serve the wider ecosystem
  • Develop cybersecurity capability
  • Create AI and automation solutions
  • Build digital products and services
  • Develop future technical and project leaders
  • Create sustainable technology ventures
  • Connect technical people across countries

The ecosystem will gradually move from:

People → Projects → Products → Ventures → International Network

03Who Can Join?

We have two primary entry pathways.

🌱 Pathway A — Beginner / Emerging Talent

For people who:

  • Have an interest in IT, technology or cybersecurity
  • May have little or no technical experience
  • Want to learn
  • Are willing to commit time
  • Want practical exposure
  • Are willing to work with a team

You do not need to be an expert. Your starting point may simply be:

“I am interested, but I don't yet know exactly where I fit.”

That's acceptable. Your journey will help you discover your strengths and develop them through structured learning and practical work.

🛡️ Pathway B — Skilled Volunteer

For people who already have:

  • Technical knowledge
  • Professional experience
  • Practical project experience
  • Specialized skills
  • Industry knowledge
  • Research capability

You may join not only to contribute your existing skills, but also to mentor emerging talent, lead technical initiatives, build products, create services, and participate in international collaboration.

Your experience can become a multiplier for the ecosystem.

04Our Core Philosophy

We believe people develop best when learning and doing happen together. Therefore, our model is not:

Learn for two years → then start working.

Instead:

Learn → Practice → Build → Receive Feedback → Improve → Contribute → Lead

Real projects will be an important part of the development process.

05What Will You Receive From Us?

If you become a member, our responsibility is not simply to give you a title. We aim to provide a development environment where you can receive:

1. Guidance

Appropriate guidance according to your level and pathway.

2. Practical Experience

Opportunities to participate in real projects rather than relying only on theory.

3. Mentorship

Where appropriate, connection with experienced members who can guide your development.

4. Team Environment

Working with people from different backgrounds and skill levels.

5. Project Opportunities

  • Software projects
  • Cybersecurity initiatives
  • AI/automation projects
  • Social impact projects
  • Service projects
  • Business/product projects

6. Leadership Opportunities

As your capability and responsibility grow: leading tasks, leading teams, training others, managing projects, leading technical initiatives.

7. Exposure to Entrepreneurship

Members who demonstrate the appropriate capability and interest may participate in developing digital products, SaaS, technology services, AI solutions, cybersecurity services, and technology ventures.

8. International Exposure

As the ecosystem develops, there may be opportunities to collaborate with people, teams, partners and projects across different countries.

06What We Expect From You

Joining the ecosystem also means accepting responsibilities. We expect every member to demonstrate:

Commitment

If you commit a certain amount of time, make a genuine effort to maintain it.

Responsibility

Complete assigned tasks and communicate when you face difficulties.

Learning Attitude

You are not expected to know everything. You are expected to be willing to learn.

Teamwork

Respect other members and contribute constructively.

Communication

Communicate clearly, especially when you cannot complete something on time.

Integrity

Be honest about your skills, experience, availability and progress.

Respect

Respect fellow members, mentors, project leaders and the wider mission of the ecosystem.

Initiative

Don't always wait for someone to tell you what to do. As you grow, we expect you to identify problems, propose solutions and take ownership.

07Your Development Journey

Our development model follows a structured progression.

  • Stage 1 — Join. You apply and go through the initial selection process.
  • Stage 2 — Counselling. We understand your current position, interests, skills, potential, availability and development goals.
  • Stage 3 — Placement. You are placed into an appropriate Track + Project + Role + Mentor.
  • Stage 4 — Development. You learn while participating in practical work.
  • Stage 5 — Contribution. You become a useful contributor to a real project.
  • Stage 6 — Leadership. With sufficient development, you may take responsibility for tasks, projects, teams, technical areas.
  • Stage 7 — Creation. You may eventually participate in creating products, services, research, technology ventures.
  • Stage 8 — Scale. Successful initiatives may expand beyond the original ecosystem to regional or international contexts.

08The Six-Month Development Framework

The six-month journey is our common development framework, not a rigid identical curriculum for everyone. A beginner and an experienced cybersecurity professional should obviously have different objectives. Therefore:

The framework is common; the journey is personalized.

Beginner

Month 1–2: Foundation + orientation · Month 3–4: Practical contribution · Month 5: Increased responsibility · Month 6: Evaluation + next-stage placement

Skilled Volunteer

Month 1–2: Integration + project placement · Month 3–4: Technical contribution / mentoring · Month 5: Ownership · Month 6: Leadership / specialist pathway evaluation

The exact objectives will be determined during counselling.

10Three Types of Projects

Not every project exists for the same purpose.

🟢 Service Projects

Technology created primarily to serve the organization/ecosystem — e.g. building a website, internal system or management platform. The primary objective is service and operational value.

🔵 Social Impact / Community-Focused Projects

Technology developed to create broader social benefit — e.g. a platform that helps coordinate food distribution, education, community services or charitable activities. The primary objective is social impact.

🟠 Business Projects

Technology developed with the potential to become a sustainable product or service — e.g. a cybersecurity service, SaaS platform or AI automation solution for businesses. The primary objective is sustainable value creation and business development.

11Project-Based Development

A project may begin small.

Local Project → Ecosystem Project → Product / Service → Regional / International Venture

This means a small project today may become something much larger tomorrow — but not every project is expected to become a business.

12Your Growth Path

Your growth is not determined only by technical skill. A possible development pathway is:

Volunteer → Ecosystem Member → Technical Contributor → Trainer / Mentor → Project Lead → Specialist Lead → Venture Builder → International Lead → Ecosystem Builder

Not everyone needs to reach every stage. Your pathway will depend on your skills, commitment, performance, interests, leadership ability, opportunities and long-term goals.

13What We Do Not Promise

We want to maintain a transparent relationship from the beginning. Joining the ecosystem does not automatically guarantee:

  • Employment
  • Salary
  • Business investment
  • A leadership position
  • International travel
  • A specific technical role or project
  • Guaranteed financial return

Opportunities will depend on the development of the ecosystem, available projects and your own demonstrated capability and commitment.

14What We Expect You to Understand

Before applying, please understand:

This is not simply a certificate programme.
This is not simply an online course.
This is not a place where members wait for instructions indefinitely.

It is a development and contribution ecosystem. You are expected to learn, participate, contribute and gradually take responsibility.

15How the Recruitment Process Works

Read This Guide → Level 1 Form → Initial Screening → Counselling → Development Placement → Begin Your Journey

Step 1 — Read This Orientation Document. Understand our vision, your responsibilities, what we provide, the development model, and the project structure.

Step 2 — Submit Level 1 Recruitment Form. We collect your basic information, interests, current experience and availability.

Step 3 — Initial Screening. Our team reviews applications.

Step 4 — Counselling. Shortlisted applicants participate in a deeper counselling process.

Step 5 — Development Placement. We determine your Track + Role + Project + Mentor + Initial Objectives.

Step 6 — Begin Your Journey. You enter the ecosystem and begin contributing according to your development plan.

16Before You Apply

Please ask yourself:

  • Am I genuinely interested in technology/cybersecurity?
  • Am I willing to learn?
  • Can I commit the time I am promising?
  • Am I willing to work with others?
  • Can I accept feedback and correction?
  • Am I willing to take responsibility?
  • Am I interested in building something useful rather than only collecting certificates?

If your answer is generally yes, we encourage you to proceed to the application process.

17Our Commitment to You

We will also hold ourselves accountable. We aim to provide:

Clarity → Guidance → Opportunity → Mentorship → Practical Experience → Feedback → Growth

We want members to know where they are going, why they are doing it and what their next step should be.

18The Bigger Vision

Our long-term objective is not simply to create a group of IT volunteers. We want to develop a connected ecosystem of Developers, Cybersecurity Specialists, AI/Data Specialists, Researchers, Product Builders, Entrepreneurs, Project Leaders and Mentors, who can contribute locally and eventually collaborate across countries.

The journey begins with an individual. Then:

Individual → Team → Project → Product → Venture → International Collaboration

19Final Message

Our goal is to help turn:

Interest into Skill → Skill into Contribution → Contribution into Leadership → Leadership into Creation → Creation into Sustainable Impact
